All distances on the site are shown as approximate, with a disclaimer.

### Vasota Fort (Koyna Wildlife Sanctuary, part of the Sahyadri Tiger Reserve)
- Reached by boat from Bamnoli, followed by a forest trek.
- Forest Department entry and photo ID are required.
- Day trips only; overnight stays on the fort are not allowed. No food or water is available on the fort.
- Generally closed in the monsoon (reported 15 June – 15 October). Reopened 1 November 2025.
- Reported 2025 fees: entry ₹100, guide ₹250, boat ₹150 per person. **These are not published on the site** because they change.

### Kaas Plateau (UNESCO World Natural Heritage, Western Ghats)
- The 2026 season opened on 1 September. Entry is reported at ₹200.
- Online booking at kas.ind.in, required at weekends. Three time slots, about 3,000 visitors a day.
- The site only says the bloom season is usually September–October and that online booking may apply.

## 6. OWNER INPUT REQUIRED

Edit these in `src/data/` and then run `npm run build`:

1. **WhatsApp number**: `WHATSAPP_NUMBER` in `hotel.js`, in international format without "+" (e.g. `91XXXXXXXXXX`). Until it is set, WhatsApp buttons open WhatsApp with the message ready and let the visitor pick the contact.
2. **Phone number**: `PHONE_NUMBER` in `hotel.js`. Until it is set, "Call Now" scrolls to the Contact section.
3. **Email, full address, postal code, map coordinates (`geo`), Google Maps link**: `hotel.js`
4. **Instagram and Facebook links**: `hotel.js`
5. **Live domain**: `siteUrl` in `hotel.js`. This is needed for the canonical URL, Open Graph image URLs and full Schema.org data.
6. **Real hotel photos** with permission: add them to `hotelPhotos` in `gallery.js` and replace the placeholders.
7. **Room types, capacity and amenities**: `rooms.js` (a template is included)
8. **Menu**: `menu.js` (categories are ready, dish lists are empty)
9. **Package inclusions and prices**: `packages.js` (prices are currently `null` and show "Get Current Price")
10. **Services the hotel actually offers**: boat booking help, guides, packed lunches, parking, and so on
11. **Genuine guest reviews**, with the guests' permission: `testimonials.js`
12. Confirm that the Justdial and Instagram listings above are really the hotel's.
